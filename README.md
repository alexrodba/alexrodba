### Hi, I'm Àlex 👋

CEO and co-founder of **[MarsBased](https://marsbased.com)**, an AI-augmented software consultancy from Barcelona, 100% remote since 2014. Angel investor. Podcaster. Musician (guitar, bass, vocals). Author of *[Camino al Exit(o)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/8417760202)*.

I write about AI, startups and running a remote company.

#### 🚀 What I'm building

- **[MarsBased](https://marsbased.com)**: 30 people building web and mobile products for startups and companies worldwide.
- **[Life on Mars](https://podcast.marsbased.com/)**: the MarsBased podcast on technology, entrepreneurship and innovation, including the *Road to CTO* series.
- **[Foc a Terra](https://focaterra.cat)**: a tech and business podcast in Catalan, co-hosted with Marc Collado.
- **[No som RES!](https://alexrodba.github.io/res/)**: punk rock in Catalan from Barcelona. I sing and play bass.
- **Angel investing**: investor in 30+ B2B startups, and LP in [itnig](https://itnig.net/) and [Masia.vc](https://masia.vc/).

#### 🛠️ Side projects

- **[No Más IBEX 35](https://nomasibex35.com/)**: alternatives to IBEX 35 companies, sector by sector. Non-profit and open source ([repo](https://github.com/alexrodba/nomasibex35)).
